The EYFS in brief

The EYFS statutory framework sets the standards for learning, development and care from birth to 5. It has seven areas of learning: three prime areas, which come first and underpin the rest, and four specific areas. Practitioners also think about three characteristics of effective teaching and learning: playing and exploring, active learning, and creating and thinking critically.

What changed on 1 September 2026

New EYFS statutory framework versions took effect. The changes include safer sleep, a requirement to have regard to screen use, and prohibiting childcare where banned dog breeds are present. Open the framework to read the exact wording for your type of provider.
